Privacy Policy

Home Privacy Policy
Last Updated: January 2026

At PWAN Properties Nigeria ("PWAN Properties", "we", "our", or "us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you visit our website, submit property enquiries, or interact with our regional offices.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal identification and property preference data from you when you interact with our platform, including:

  • Contact Information: Full name, email address, telephone number, and residential address.
  • Enquiry Details: Preferred locations, property types (duplex, apartment, land), budget ranges, and site inspection booking dates.
  • Technical Data: Browser type, IP address, device information, and saved property favorites stored locally via browser storage (`localStorage`).

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is utilized strictly for professional real estate and customer service operations, including:

  • Processing property purchase, rental, or land investment enquiries.
  • Scheduling physical site inspections and virtual tours.
  • Sending requested estate brochures, market updates, and property catalog responses.
  • Improving website performance, navigation experience, and responsive layouts.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification data to third parties. Information is only shared with authorized internal real estate consultants and legal teams when necessary to fulfill property allocation or inspection requests, or when required by Nigerian regulatory authorities.

4. Security of Your Information

We implement appropriate technical, administrative, and physical security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no data transmission over the internet can be guaranteed 100% secure.

5. Your Data Protection Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data you have provided through our contact or property submission forms. You may also clear saved property favorites stored in your browser at any time.
