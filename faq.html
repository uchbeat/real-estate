Home FAQs

Purchasing a property with PWAN Properties involves four simple steps: 1) Select your desired listing online or consult with an advisor, 2) Book and conduct a site inspection, 3) Complete our offer/application form and submit legal ID verification, 4) Complete your payment schedule and receive your legal title documents and deed of assignment.

Our land estates come with verified legal documentations depending on the location, including Certificate of Ownership (C of O), Governor’s Consent, Excision, Gazette, or Registered Survey titles. All documents are vetted by our in-house legal team before public release.

Yes, we offer flexible payment structures ranging from 3 to 12 months for selected residential houses and land estates. An initial deposit locks in your plot allocation, while the remaining balance is spread across agreed monthly intervals.

Physical allocation dates are scheduled immediately upon completion of 100% full payment or upon reaching agreed threshold deposits for specified estates. Surveyors issue beacon numbers and physical coordinates during official allocation batches.

Standard rental payments in Nigeria typically include the Annual Basic Rent, Caution Deposit (refundable), Legal Agreement Fee (usually 10%), Agency Fee (10%), and Estate Service Charges for maintenance where applicable. All charges are itemized prior to signing lease contracts.

Absolutely. We specialize in diaspora real estate acquisitions. We provide live virtual video tours, digital document signing, verified bank transfer channels, and allow designated local representatives to conduct physical inspections on your behalf.

A Certificate of Occupancy (C of O) is the first legal document issued by the State Government confirming 99-year lease ownership of virgin land. A Governor's Consent is required whenever a land plot with an existing C of O or legal title is subsequently sold or transferred to a new buyer.
